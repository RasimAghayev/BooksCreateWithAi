You can think of maps as a bit like a dictionary, where definitions (values)
are retrieved by looking up the word (key).
Maps are valuable data structures because they provide a fast way to take a
key, such as a string or or a struct, and use it as an index to store and retrieve
values from the map.

Internally, maps are implemented using hash tables, which contain a
collection of buckets. When you’re storing, removing, or looking up a
key/value pair, everything starts with selecting a bucket.
