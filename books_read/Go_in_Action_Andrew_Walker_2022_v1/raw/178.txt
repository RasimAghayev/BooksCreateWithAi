4.4 Summary
Arrays are constant-sized collections of data that form the basis of the
slice and map types.
Slices are powerful and flexible ordered collection types, and the
primary ordered collection type in Go.
append works by returning a new slice header, though it does not always
allocate new storage.
Maps are Go’s associative array or "dictionary" type, and allow you to
store values of any type using a key type best suited for your use-case.
Slices and maps are fundamentally reference types that point to the data
they contain, and it helps to be mindful of how they work and when you
should modify them.
