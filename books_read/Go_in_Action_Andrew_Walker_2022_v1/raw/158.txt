By adding a length check, you ensure that accessing an invalid entry is
impossible, since the function will just return an error otherwise. It also gives
you the opportunity to log a helpful error message with the name of the
invalid condition and the number of arguments you actually got. Of course,
this means every call to your function needs to do error checking to ensure
that nothing went wrong, and this function is pretty inflexible, since races
with less than three people are invalid.
