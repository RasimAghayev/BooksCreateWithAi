For this, you can use a slice expression with three indices provided in the
form of a[low : high : max]. In this form, the capacity of the new slice
will cut off at the max index, preventing any modification of the original slice
beyond this point.
Listing 4.50. Slice Expressions With And Without A max Value
intSlice = []int{1, 2, 3, 4, 5}
newSliceA = intSlice[:2] // [1 2]
newSliceB = intSlice[:2:3] // [1 2] (same values, less capacity)
