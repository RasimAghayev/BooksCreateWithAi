How Much Will append Grow The Capacity?
The append operation is clever when growing the capacity of the backing
array. As of this printing, the algoritm will always double the existing
capacity if the total number of elements less than 1,024. Past this number, the
capacity is grown by a factor of .25, or 25%, though this could change in
future versions of the language as more optimizations are added.
