syntactic style that could span weeks. It was all very fraught and dramatic
and a lot of good development time was burnt debating the merits of variable
grouping and cuddled else statements. Ain’t no one got time for that.
With standard formatting, it doesn’t matter what your preference might be.
What style of bracketing should you use? Doesn’t matter, it’s gofmt’s way.
It’s actually rather freeing, since now you just write your code and you don’t
worry about it. Moreover, standard formatting lowers the cognitive load for
reading unfamiliar code since at the very least the shape of it will be the
same. It’s great.
If you’re unsure about whether your editor is calling gofmt for you, just try
the following command on your shiny new main.go:
Listing 2.6. Testing Your Formatting With gofmt
$ gofmt -d main.go
