You can see that we now have a sync.WaitGroup, onto which we add a delta of 1 to the counter. Inside
the goroutine we decrement the counter with defer wg.Done(), then we block until the counter is
zero with wg.Wait(). Since we’re blocking until the goroutine finishes, there is no longer a data race:
