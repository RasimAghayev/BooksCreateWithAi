Generating Coverage Reports
To generate test coverage percentages for your code, simply run the go test -cover command. Let’s
make a quick example and a test to go with it.
We’re going to write a simple username validation function. We want our usernames to only contain
letters, numbers, and the special characters “-“, “_”, and “.”. Usernames also cannot be empty, and
they must be less than 30 characters long. Here’s our username validation function:
