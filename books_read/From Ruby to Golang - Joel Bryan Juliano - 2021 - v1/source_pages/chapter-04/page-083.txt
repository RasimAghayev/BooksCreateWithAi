C-style Semantic Form
Probably the most prominent for loop example, the C style semantic form. This initializes i := 0
as the initial index, then adds a condition that if the index i is less than the length of fruits, i <
len(fruits) the for loop will continually run, the last statement continually iterates one to i index
then stores the sum to itself, via i++.

Questions
1. What have we learned?
The for loop is a control structure that allows us to iterate over a range of values.
2. Why do we use C-style semantic form in Go?
It is the most common form of for loop, and is used in many languages.
