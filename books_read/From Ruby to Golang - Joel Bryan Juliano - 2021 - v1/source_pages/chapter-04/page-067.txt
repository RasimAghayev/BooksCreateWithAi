As you noticed, upon printing the values of both arrays, on line 16 and 17, the values of both
arrays are independent of each other. We both inserted strings on both arrays and they both have
independent values. This is the expected behavior of a Fixed Array being a value type, however, this
can get really expensive on large data sets, and for this scenarios, we would require a flexible type
of array, the Sliced Array.
