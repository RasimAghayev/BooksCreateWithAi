Adopting service discovery
71
You may notice that instead of calling a static pre-configured address, we now first get the 
available addresses of the metadata from the registry. This is the essence of service discovery 
– we use the data from the registry for making remote calls between our services. After we get 
the list of service addresses, we pick a random one using a rand.Intn function. By doing 
this, we balance the load between the active instances, randomly selecting any available instance 
on each request.
