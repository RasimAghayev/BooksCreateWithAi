What is a microservice?
Companies worldwide have used the microservice architecture model so widely that it has almost 
become a default way of software development. Those companies have tens, hundreds, and even 
thousands of microservices at their disposal.
So, what exactly is the microservice model?
The microservice architecture model is organizing an application as a collection of services, called 
microservices, each of which is further responsible for a certain part of application logic, usually 
defined by a particular business capability. 
As an example, consider an online marketplace application. The application may have multiple 
features, including search, shopping cart, payments, order history, and many more. Each feature can 
be so different that the code may (and, in certain cases, should) be completely independent of the 
rest of the application. In this example, search and payments technically have nothing in common.
