About the reviewers
Aaron Torres received his master's of science degree in computer science from
New Mexico Institute of Mining and Technology. He has worked on distributed
systems in high-performance computing and in large-scale web and
microservices applications.
Aaron has published a number of papers and has several patents in the areas of
storage and I/O. He is passionate about sharing his knowledge and ideas with
others. He is also a fan of the Go language and open source for backend systems
and development.
 
Vishal Rana is the creator of the Echo web framework and many other popular
open source projects. Currently, he is working as a staff engineer for PayPal,
where he is involved in building distributed and scalable systems to process
millions of events per day using Go, REST, Java, Docker, microservices,
Hadoop, Kafka, Spark, Elasticsearch, and other open source technologies.
