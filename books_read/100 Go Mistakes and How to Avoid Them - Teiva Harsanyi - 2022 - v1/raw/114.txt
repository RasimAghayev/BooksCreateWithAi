CHAPTER 3
Data types
A map can always grow in memory, but it never shrinks. Hence, if it leads to
some memory issues, you can try different options, such as forcing Go to re-
create the map or using pointers.
To compare types in Go, you can use the == and != operators if two types are
comparable: Booleans, numerals, strings, pointers, channels, and structs are
composed entirely of comparable types. Otherwise, you can either use
reflect.DeepEqual and pay the price of reflection or use custom implementa-
tions and libraries.
