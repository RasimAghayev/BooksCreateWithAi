Sealing the Footer
The footer, like the header, remains relatively consistent, housing
information like contact details, terms of service, and social media links.
{{define "footer"}}
<div class="footer">
   <p>Contact us at contact@gitforgits.com</p>
   <ul class="social-media">
       <li>Facebook</li>
       <li>Twitter</li>
   </ul>
</div>
{{end}}
To construct a complete webpage, these nested templates can be rendered
within the base template. Using Go's template execution functions, specific
content templates (like "booklist") can be injected into the base, forming a
complete, structured webpage.
