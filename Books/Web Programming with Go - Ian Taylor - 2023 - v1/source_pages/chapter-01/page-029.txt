While Go's standard toolchain is comprehensive, developers sometimes
need to dive deeper or access underlying tools. The go tool command offers
this flexibility, providing direct access to tools like the compiler, linker, and
trace. For developers seeking granular control or wanting to explore the
internals of the Go toolchain, this command is invaluable. This entire
toolchain not only simplifies tasks but also ingrains best practices,
epitomizing Go's philosophy of simplicity and effectiveness.
