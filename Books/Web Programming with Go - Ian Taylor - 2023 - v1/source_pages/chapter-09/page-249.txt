   return m.reviews[bookID], nil
}
Mocking provides a controlled environment for your tests. You don't have
to worry about external services being down or the internet being slow.
Every test run is consistent, making failures easier to diagnose. However,
it's crucial to remember that while mocks simulate external behaviors, they
might not capture every nuance of the real thing. It's still essential to have
integration tests, in addition to unit tests, to ensure all parts of your system
work together correctly.
