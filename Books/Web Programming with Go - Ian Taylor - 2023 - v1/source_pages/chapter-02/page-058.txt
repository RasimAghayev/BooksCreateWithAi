using vendoring, the external packages that are associated with the project

The packages in the /vendor directory are given higher priority by Go
during the application's construction process than those in the global
GOPATH directory.

Benefits of Vendoring
Your project is isolated from system-wide packages, ensuring
that updates or removals in the global space don't affect your
project.
