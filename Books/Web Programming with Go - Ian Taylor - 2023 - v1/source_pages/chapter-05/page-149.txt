   book_id INT REFERENCES books(id),
   stock_quantity INT NOT NULL,
   restock_threshold INT,
   last_restock_date DATE
);
This schema aids in tracking stock levels and can trigger restock alerts
when quantities dip below the threshold. The proposed schema ensures
every detail, every transaction, and every review is captured, processed, and
presented, ensuring the bookstore's operations are smooth, efficient, and
insightful.
