Advanced ORMs provide middleware and hooks, allowing developers to
tap into the lifecycle of data operations. For GitforGits, it could mean
validating a new user's data before it's saved or modifying a book's details
before an update operation. These hooks ensure data integrity and allow for
advanced functionalities without the added complexity of manual SQL
triggers or procedures.
