GitforGits, users might want to view reviews of a specific book written
after a certain date, such as:
URL: gitforgits.com/book/5678/reviews?after=2023-01-01
Both the book ID (path parameter) and the date (query parameter) influence
the content displayed. By understanding and implementing dynamic URL
parameters, developers empower the bookstore to be more than a static
platform. It becomes a fluid, responsive entity, always ready to cater to each
user's unique literary journey.
