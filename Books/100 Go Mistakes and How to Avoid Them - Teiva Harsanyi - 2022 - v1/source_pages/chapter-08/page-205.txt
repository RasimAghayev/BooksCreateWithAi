Here, the OS scheduler decided to move M2 to P2 and M3 to P3. Again, there is no

our four-thread application, this should be the final picture.
 The situation has changed; it has become optimal. The four goroutines are run-
ning in separate threads and the threads on separate cores. This approach reduces
the amount of context switching at both the goroutine and thread levels.
