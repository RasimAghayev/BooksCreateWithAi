In Go, a slice grows by doubling its size until it contains 1,024 ele-

The slice now references the new backing array. What will happen to the previous
backing array? If it’s no longer referenced, it’s eventually freed by the garbage collec-
tor (GC) if allocated on the heap. (We discuss heap memory in mistake #95, “Not
understanding stack vs. heap,” and we look at how the GC works in mistake #99, “Not
understanding how the GC works.”)
