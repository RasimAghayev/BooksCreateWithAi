BenchmarkPopcnt2 is the accurate version of the benchmark. It guarantees that we
avoid the inlining optimizations, which can artificially lower the execution time or
even remove the call to the function under test. Relying on the results of Benchmark-
Popcnt1 could have led to wrong assumptions.
 Let’s remember the pattern to avoid compiler optimizations fooling benchmark
results: assign the result of the function under test to a local variable, and then assign
the latest result to a global variable. This best practice also prevents us from making
incorrect assumptions.
