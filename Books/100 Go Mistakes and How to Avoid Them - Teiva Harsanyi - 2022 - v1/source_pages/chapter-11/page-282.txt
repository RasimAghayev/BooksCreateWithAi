Testing
Testing is a crucial aspect of a project’s lifecycle. It offers countless benefits, such as
building confidence in an application, acting as code documentation, and making
refactoring easier. Compared to some other languages, Go has strong primitives for
writing tests. Throughout this chapter, we look at common mistakes that make the
testing process brittle, less effective, and less accurate.
