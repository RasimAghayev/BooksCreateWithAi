Struct (Record)
In computer science, a record (also called
a structure, struct, or compound data) is
a basic data structure. A record is a
collection of fields, possibly of different
data types, typically in fixed number and
sequence. The fields of a record may also
be called members, particularly in
object-oriented programming; fields may
also be called elements, though these risk
confusion with the elements of a
collection. Records are distinguished
from arrays by the fact that their
number of fields is typically fixed, each
field has a name, and that each field may
have a different type. A record type is a
data type that describes such values and
variables.

Subroutine
In computer programming, a subroutine
is a sequence of program instructions
that performs a specific task, packaged
as a unit. This unit can then be used in
programs wherever that particular task
should be performed.
