Accessibility
Accessibility in the sense considered here
refers to the design of products, devices,
services, or environments so as to be
usable by people with disabilities. The
concept of accessible design and practice
of accessible development ensures both
“direct access” (i.e. unassisted) and
“indirect access” meaning compatibility
with a person’s assistive technology (for
example, computer screen readers).

Accessor (Mutator method)
In computer science, a mutator method
is a method used to control changes to a
variable. They are also widely known as
setter methods. Often a setter is
accompanied by a getter (also known as
an accessor), which returns the value of
the private member variable.
