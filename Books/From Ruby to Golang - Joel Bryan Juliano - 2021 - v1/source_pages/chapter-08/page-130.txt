Documentation (general)
is a set of documents provided on paper,
or online, or on digital or analog media,
such as audio tape or CDs. Examples are
user guides, white papers, on-line help,
quick-reference guides. It is becoming
less common to see paper (hard-copy)
documentation. Documentation is
distributed via websites, software
products, and other on-line applications.
