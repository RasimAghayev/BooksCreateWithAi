Further Reading
The Go Programming Language Specification⁵⁰
Effective Go⁵¹ is a good overview document for how to write idiomatic Go code.
Golang Weekly⁵² is a weekly newsletter about Go.
The Go Blog⁵³ is the official blog for Go.
Gophers Slack⁵⁴ is a Slack community of Go developers. There is a #golang-newbies channel,
#performance, #golang-jobs, and many more.
