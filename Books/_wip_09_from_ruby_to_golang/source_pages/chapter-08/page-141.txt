Initialization (Programming)
In computer programming, initialization
(or initialisation) is the assignment of an
initial value for a data object or variable.
The manner in which initialization is
performed depends on programming
language, as well as type, storage class,
etc., of an object to be initialized.
Programming constructs which perform
initialization are typically called
initializers and initializer lists.
Initialization is distinct from (and
preceded by) declaration, although the
two can sometimes be conflated in
practice. The complement of
initialization is finalization, which is
primarily used for objects, but not
variables.
