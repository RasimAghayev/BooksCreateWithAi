Calculate
To determine the value of something or
the solution to something by a
mathematical process
Calculation
A calculation is a deliberate process that
transforms one or more inputs into one
or more results, with variable change.
The term is used in a variety of senses,
from the very definite arithmetical
calculation of using an algorithm, to the
vague heuristics of calculating a strategy
in a competition, or calculating the
chance of a successful relationship
between two people.
Call
A telephone conversation, in computing,
this can be a function call to other
functions, or a remote procedure call to
other services
Capability
The power or ability to generate an
outcome. A capability is the ability to do
things and to choose for a way of life
according to one’s personal values. As it
applies to human capital, capability
represents performing or achieving
certain actions/ outcomes in terms of the
intersection of capacity and ability.
