Testing microservices
Microservice architecture encourages you to write minimal services that can be easily
tested as a unit. For example, while testing the Order service, the only context you
need to focus on is Order. To test the Order service, you have two high-level alterna-
tives: manual testing and automated testing.
 With manual testing, you must run actual and dependent services to test the entire
flow. This can be a time-consuming operation compared to testing backed by a
machine. This inefficient testing methodology will also slow down your software deliv-
ery. However, with automated testing, you can get fast feedback from your implemen-
tation because you don’t need to wait until you finish and test the entire system.
 We will look at different testing strategies in this chapter, but first let’s look at
the relations between them and their possible advantages and disadvantages.
