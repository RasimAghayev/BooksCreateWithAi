JAEGER SPM
In SPM, you can see the performance monitoring metrics for each service after click-
ing the Monitor menu in Jaeger UI. To understand the basic performance analysis of
the Order service, select Order in the dropdown menu to see a performance sum-
mary that contains p95 latency, request rate, error rate, and so on (figure 9.14).
In figure 9.14, the p95 latency of the Order service is 46.79 ms, meaning 95% of the
latencies are 46.79 ms or less. You can also see the frequency of the requests: 6 per sec-
ond. As a reminder, these performance metrics are aggregated from Prometheus.
